The walls, the gutters, the roof. Two companies.

Rocklane Co. installs gutters, siding and metal wall panels on warehouses, apartment and condo buildings and commercial exteriors. Low-slope membrane roofs are the work of a separate company: Rocklane Commercial Contractors LLC.

04 Property managers & building owners

One call for the whole building.

You manage buildings, not contractor org charts. Here is how a commercial or multifamily exterior project moves.

  1. You call the officeOwners are active in daily management. If something goes wrong, you get an owner on the phone.
  2. The scope is split by companyGutters, siding and metal wall panels go to Rocklane Co.. A low-slope membrane roof goes to Rocklane Commercial Contractors LLC. Both are part of the Rocklane Group.
  3. Specialists when the building needs themStorm-damage inspectors, public adjusters and engineers from our specialist network. You never manage the handoff.
  4. One record of the workStatus, updates, photos and documents for Rocklane Co. projects in the customer portal.
