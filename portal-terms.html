Customer Portal Terms of Use

Rocklane Company General Contractors LLC · Updated November 6, 2026

The Rocklane Co. customer portal lets our customers follow their projects, share photos and documents with our team, and view project videos. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

Your account

  • Accounts are for Rocklane Co. customers, property managers and board members with a project we are performing or have performed.
  • Keep your password private and don’t share your account. Tell us right away at (317) 898-8696 if you think someone else has used it.
  • We give access to a project only to the email addresses connected to it. We may change or remove access when a project’s contacts change.

Files you upload

  • Upload only photos and documents related to your project that you have the right to share.
  • Files are visible to our team and to the other contacts on the same project.
  • Don’t upload payment card numbers, Social Security numbers or other sensitive personal information.

Project information and videos

Project status, updates and videos are provided for your convenience. Your signed contract, estimate and warranty documents govern your project. Project videos are for your use in connection with your property and may not be republished without our permission.

Availability

We work to keep the portal available but cannot guarantee it will always be. If you can’t reach it, call the office at (317) 898-8696.
