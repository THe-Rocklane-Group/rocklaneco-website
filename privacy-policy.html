Privacy Policy

Rocklane Company General Contractors LLC · Updated November 6, 2026

What we collect

  • Estimate requests. Your name, email, phone number, property address and the project details you send through our contact form, by phone or by email.
  • Customer portal accounts. Your name, email, phone number, company or HOA name and property address, plus a password that is stored only in encrypted form by Google Firebase. We never see your password.
  • Project files. Photos and documents you or our team upload to your project.
  • Portal activity. A record of sign-ins, uploads and downloads, kept for security and to support you.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to you, prepare estimates, carry out and document your project, give you access to the customer portal, and keep the portal secure. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use advertising trackers.

Who stores it

Portal accounts and project records are stored with Google Firebase. Estimate requests, emails and project files are stored in our Google Workspace account. Project videos are hosted on YouTube as unlisted videos, shown in the portal only to the customers on that project. Our pages load the Montserrat typeface from Google Fonts, and the map on our contact page is provided by Google. Google may collect technical information under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and storage

The public website sets no cookies of its own. When you sign in to the customer portal, your browser stores a sign-in token so you stay signed in. Uncheck “Keep me signed in” on shared computers. The portal signs you out after 60 minutes without activity.

How long we keep it

We keep project records and files for as long as needed for your project, its warranty period and our legal and tax obligations. You may ask us to close your portal account at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, or to close your portal account, contact us at rocklane@rocklaneco.com or (317) 898-8696.
