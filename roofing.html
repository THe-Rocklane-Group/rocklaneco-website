07 Know the warning signs

Signs a roof needs attention.

Spot any of these? Ask for an owner-led inspection. If a repair will outperform a replacement, we’ll tell you.

  • Curling, cracked or missing shingles
  • Shingle granules collecting in the gutters
  • Water stains on ceilings or in the attic
  • Sagging areas or daylight through the roof deck
  • Hail or wind damage after a storm
