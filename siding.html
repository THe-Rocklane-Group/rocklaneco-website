01 Replacement · Repair · Inspection

Siding that protects the building envelope.

Siding is the first layer of protection for every wall. We install it to keep water out for decades, and we back our siding work with a 5-year workmanship warranty.

  • Siding replacement: James Hardie® fiber cement or Variform vinyl siding, installed with precise flashing and trim details.
  • Siding repair: storm, impact and moisture damage repaired and matched to the existing exterior.
  • Inspections: we identify problem areas before they turn into rot, mold or interior damage.
Compare fiber cement and vinyl
02Fiber cement vs. vinyl

Two materials. One crew standard.

We install both, with the same Rocklane crews and the same workmanship warranty. Here is how they compare, so you can choose the right siding for your building.

Fiber cement

James Hardie® siding

We install James Hardie® siding: rigid fiber cement boards with crisp lines and a painted finish, a strong choice when you want the look of painted wood with very little movement.

Vinyl

Variform vinyl siding

Vinyl siding that stands up to Indiana weather with very little upkeep, in lap and shake profiles, a practical choice for single homes and whole communities.

Fiber cement and vinyl siding at a glance
FeatureJames Hardie® fiber cementVariform vinyl
MaterialFiber cement boards made from cement, sand and cellulose fibersVinyl siding panels
LookCrisp, deep lines with a painted finishLap and shake profiles in a wide range of colors
UpkeepLow; the painted finish may need refreshing over the long termVery little; no painting, an occasional wash
InstallationHeavier boards cut and fastened with fiber-cement toolingLighter panels hung to allow for seasonal expansion and contraction
Often chosen forHomes where a rigid, painted-wood look matters mostHomes and multi-building communities that want low upkeep

03How we install

The details decide how long siding lasts.

Water gets behind siding at its edges and openings. That is where our crews take the most care.

  • Flashing

    Precise flashing at windows, doors and rooflines, so water is turned away from the wall.

  • Trim details

    Trim fitted cleanly around corners and openings, finishing the wall and closing the gaps.

  • Matched repairs

    Storm, impact and moisture damage repaired and matched to the existing exterior.

  • Problem areas found early

    We look for trouble spots before they turn into rot, mold or interior damage.
